Mini World Ideas

        
      

      

        
Balanced Garden
3 plants, 2 munchers, normal food growth.

        
Hungry World
1 plant, 5 munchers, slow food growth.

        
Magnet City
4 drifters and 2 beacons with strong beacon pull.

        
Maro Fan Club
Make creatures follow Maro and walk around the stage.

        
Island World
Use ponds to create slow zones and place food between them.

        
Your Own Experiment
Build any world, then change exactly one rule and compare.
